## 1. Geometric Dimensioning and Tolerancing (GD&T) Review
The 2D technical drawing establishes the precise manufacturing controls for the Al 6061-T6 aluminum part. Key geometric controls include:
* **Form & Profile:** A profile of a surface tolerance of 0.1 is applied relative to datum B to control the external conical and cylindrical boundaries. A flatness tolerance of 0.1 is also specified.
* **Position:** A position tolerance of 0.1 is utilized to constrain specific feature locations accurately. 
* **Overall Dimensions:** The overall length dimensions include segments of 650 mm and 350 mm, with specific radial elements (R10) defining the dimpled or filleted geometries.

## 2. Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) Post-Processing Results
A multi-physics fluid flow analysis was conducted to evaluate the aerodynamic boundary layer behavior across the dimpled geometry.

### Pressure Distribution
* **Contour 1 (Pressure):** The pressure field acting on the rocket ranges from a minimum of -4.289e+03 Pa to a maximum stagnation pressure of 2.328e+03 Pa. 

### Velocity Profile
* **Contour 4 (Global Velocity):** The surface and near-field velocity contour shows a maximum velocity of 1.218e+02 m/s. 
* **Streamline 1 (3D Velocity Streamlines):** Tracking from the inlet, the fluid velocity streamlines accelerate up to 1.230e+02 m/s as the flow navigates the dimpled body. 

### Turbulence and Viscosity
* **Contour 2 (Eddy Viscosity):** The simulation reveals an eddy viscosity peak of 4.328e-02 Pa s, indicating regions of turbulent mixing generated by the dimples.
* **Contour 3 (Specific Dissipation Rate):** The specific dissipation rate reaches a maximum of 1.152e+05 s^-1.

## 3. Conclusion
The combination of strict position and profile tolerances ensures the dimple geometry is manufactured exactly as simulated. The localized pressure drops and eddy viscosity fields demonstrate the aerodynamic impact of the dimpled pattern on boundary layer turbulence and flow separation.
